## 🏆 HALO TOURNAMENT FORMAT

Based on previous DGR LAN scoring (2021 & 2023):

- **15 total games** across the weekend
- **FFA games** — points awarded by finishing position (1st = best)
- **Team games** — winning team splits bonus points
- **Lowest cumulative score wins** (like golf scoring from prior events)
- Leaderboard posted after each block
- **Grand Champion** gets eternal Discord glory 🏅
