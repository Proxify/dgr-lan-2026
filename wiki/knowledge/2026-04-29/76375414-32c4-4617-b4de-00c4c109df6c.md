# Ranger and Mage class colors added to squad cards

The `classColors` and `classPixelColors` maps in `SquadMemberCard.tsx` were missing entries for Ranger and Mage, causing both to fall back to the default blue. Ranger now renders in teal (Wind icon) and Mage in fuchsia (Sparkles icon).
